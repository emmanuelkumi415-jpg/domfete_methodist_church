About Domfete Society

Who We Are

Domfete Society is a growing Christian community within the Jinijini Circuit of the Methodist Church Ghana. We are committed to worship, discipleship, evangelism and community transformation through Jesus Christ.

Founded by devoted early members including Abraham Kyere and Isaac Febiri, the Church experienced revival in the late 1960s and has continued to grow spiritually and numerically to the glory of God.

Our Mission

To make disciples of Jesus Christ through worship, teaching, evangelism and compassionate service to our community.

Our Vision

To be a vibrant and spirit-filled church that transforms lives, strengthens families and impacts society for Christ.

Our Core Values

  • Biblical Teaching
  • Prayer & Worship
  • Holiness & Integrity
  • Love & Unity
  • Community Service
